Candles

Torch

1. Your torch holds the ghost still. The moment your light leaves it, it moves. Keep it in your beam.
2. Each room hides Elias’s ledger page. It has a red wax seal and glows red. Find it first.
3. Press L or tap Ledger to open it. It shows a map of the room with every candle numbered in the order to light it.
4. Stand beside a candle until its ring fills. Wrong order and every flame goes out.
5. Batteries refill your torch. Light every candle to open the door.
